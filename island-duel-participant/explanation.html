Island Duel evaluation evidence

Requires Python 3.10+ and the native engine from the participant bundle. No third-party Python packages are required.

The policies rank legal actions by score gain, energy cost, and expansion potential. Later policies estimate the strongest visible countercapture, reward affordable follow-up moves, and adjust play as the end of the match approaches.

Qualification requires 308 wins across 512 fresh games and wins in at least 24 groups.
